# Market prices – loaded 2026-10-19

Prices per kg used by the system from 2026-10-19. Grade A is shown; Grade B = 85% and Grade C = 70% of Grade A.

**Purchase** = what the collection centre pays the farmer. **Selling** = what buyers pay. purchase = price paid to the farmer at the collection centre (about 90% of the median wholesale price across Sri Lankan economic centres); selling = price to buyers (about 115% of that median)

Sources:
- **HARTI** – Hector Kobbekaduwa Agrarian Research and Training Institute – Daily Food Commodities Bulletin, wholesale prices 16–18 Oct 2026 (Peliyagoda, Dambulla, Meegoda, Keppetipola, Nuwara Eliya, Bandarawela, Thambuththegama, Veyangoda, Norochchole)
- **CBSL** – Central Bank of Sri Lanka – Daily Price Report, 16 Oct 2026 (Pettah, Dambulla, Marandagahamula wholesale; Pettah, Dambulla, Narahenpita retail)
- **Estimate** – Not covered by the official daily bulletins – a market estimate; please confirm with your collection centre before use
